# System Name Proposal

## Recommended name

**SILA Commerce OS — صِلَة**

> **صِلَة** expresses the relationship that joins customer, merchant, courier, creator, and the local market without implying that the platform owns or holds merchant funds.

This is the strongest fit for the current product. It is short, pronounceable in Arabic and English, broad enough for a modular commerce operating system, and aligned with the Yemen-first principle of connecting existing local business practices rather than forcing every merchant into one payment or fulfilment model. The English-facing form should be written as **SILA**, while the Arabic-facing mark should be **صِلَة** with the diacritic omitted in compact UI contexts when necessary: **صلة**.

| Criterion | Why SILA / صِلَة fits |
|---|---|
| Arabic-first | The Arabic word is familiar, positive, and easy to render in RTL interfaces. |
| Yemen-first | It suggests trusted local relationships between merchants, customers, couriers, and community markets. |
| Modular scope | It does not limit the product to a storefront, wallet, courier, or payment provider. |
| Governance and trust | It communicates connection and accountability without promising custody, financing, or automated provider settlement. |
| Product architecture | **SILA Commerce OS** works for the customer app, merchant workspace, Creator Console, APIs, and future modules. |

## Suggested product hierarchy

Use **SILA** as the master brand, **SILA Market** for the customer marketplace, **SILA Merchant** for the merchant workspace, and **SILA Console** for the creator-management application. Keep the technical repository name `yemen-commerce` until branding approval, trademark screening, and domain decisions are complete.

## Alternatives

| Name | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **BUNYAN — بنيان** | Conveys a durable commerce foundation and ecosystem building. | More architectural and less immediately marketplace-oriented. |
| **MIHWAR — مِحور** | Clearly communicates a central modular hub. | Feels more infrastructural and less warm for customer-facing commerce. |
| **SOUQNA — سوقنا** | Directly communicates “our market” and local ownership. | More generic and narrower than the operating-system ambition. |

Before public launch, perform trademark, company-name, domain, app-store, and social-handle checks in Yemen and target export markets. This proposal does not claim that any name or domain is legally or commercially available.
