# System Name Proposal

## Research status

The earlier **SILA Commerce OS — صِلَة** proposal was rejected after web research because Sila/صلة is already used by ecommerce, POS, payments, MENA market intelligence, and Lebanese agricultural organizations. A later BUNYAN proposal is also **not approved**: the new research found direct Yemeni and regional conflicts, especially مؤسسة بنيان التنموية and بنيان سوفت. See [`BUNYAN_NAME_RESEARCH.md`](./BUNYAN_NAME_RESEARCH.md) for the evidence.

## Earlier candidate: SILA

**SILA Commerce OS — صِلَة**

> **صِلَة** expresses the relationship that joins customer, merchant, courier, creator, and the local market without implying that the platform owns or holds merchant funds.

This was an earlier conceptual fit only and is **not an approved name**. Web research found substantial existing uses of SILA/صلة across ecommerce, POS, payments, consumer intelligence, and other regional businesses. It must not be presented as available or recommended; see [`SILA_NAME_RESEARCH.md`](./SILA_NAME_RESEARCH.md).

| Criterion | Why SILA / صِلَة fits |
|---|---|
| Arabic-first | The Arabic word is familiar, positive, and easy to render in RTL interfaces. |
| Yemen-first | It suggests trusted local relationships between merchants, customers, couriers, and community markets. |
| Modular scope | It does not limit the product to a storefront, wallet, courier, or payment provider. |
| Governance and trust | It communicates connection and accountability without promising custody, financing, or automated provider settlement. |
| Product architecture | **SILA Commerce OS** works for the customer app, merchant workspace, Creator Console, APIs, and future modules. |

## Interim product hierarchy

Keep `yemen-commerce` as the technical and public working name. Do not relabel the customer app, merchant workspace, or Creator Console as SILA or BUNYAN. After a new distinctive candidate is selected and screened, the product hierarchy can use a master brand with separate Market, Merchant, and Console surfaces.

## Earlier alternatives

| Name | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **BUNYAN — بنيان** | Conveys a durable commerce foundation and ecosystem building. | **Rejected for now:** direct Yemeni name conflict and regional technology/ecommerce conflicts; see [`BUNYAN_NAME_RESEARCH.md`](./BUNYAN_NAME_RESEARCH.md). |
| **MIHWAR — مِحور** | Clearly communicates a central modular hub. | Feels more infrastructural and less warm for customer-facing commerce. |
| **SOUQNA — سوقنا** | Directly communicates “our market” and local ownership. | More generic and narrower than the operating-system ambition. |

Before public launch, perform trademark, company-name, domain, app-store, and social-handle checks in Yemen and target export markets. The current decision is to keep `yemen-commerce` as the technical and public working name until a new shortlist is researched and legally screened. Neither SILA nor BUNYAN should be treated as approved or commercially available.
